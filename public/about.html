How articles are written here

1
One clear outcome per article

Every piece starts with a reader outcome, not a topic. You should be able to do, decide, or understand something specific after reading.

2
Concrete over abstract

One real example is worth ten vague explanations. If a concept can't be shown with a specific case, it's not ready to be written.

3
No filler

If a sentence doesn't move the reader forward, it gets cut. Articles here are as long as they need to be — not a word more.

4
Always a next step

Every article ends with something actionable. Reading without doing is just entertainment. The goal is always to be useful.
